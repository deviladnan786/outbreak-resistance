# OPTION 1: *BULLET FORGE* (Weapon Evolution / Math-Gate Runner)

> **The 2-Second Concept**: Your gun glides forward automatically. You only steer **Left and Right**. Shoot incoming crates and pass through blue math gates (`+5 Fire Rate`, `x2 Bullets`) while dodging red gates (`-50 Damage`). At the finish line, unleash all your bullets into a giant gold vault.  
> **Why Anyone Understands It**: There are zero tutorial popups needed. Blue numbers = good. Red numbers = bad. Shoot target = earn coins.

```
[START MENU] ──> [RUNNER TRACK (30-45s)] ──> [VAULT BLAST FINALE] ──> [REWARD/UPGRADE]
                                                                          │
                                                                          └──> [NEXT LEVEL]
```

### Complete Game Scope (What the Full Game Contains)
1. **Controls**: Mouse drag or A/D keys (PC) / Touch drag (Mobile) to steer left/right. Auto-fires continuously.
2. **Track Elements**:
   * **Math Gates**: Floating energy gates. Blue (`+10 Fire Rate`, `x2 Barrels`, `+20 Range`), Red (`-5 Fire Rate`, `÷2 Barrels`).
   * **Destructible Blocks**: Numbered obstacle blocks (e.g., "50 HP"). Shooting them down gives cash and clears the path.
   * **Money Piles**: Glowing cash scattered on lanes to steer into.
3. **Finish Line (The Vault Blast)**:
   * The track ends at a row of 10 armored bank vaults with increasing multipliers ($1.5x$, $2x$, $5x$, $10x$).
   * Your gun unleashes maximum rapid-fire with whatever stats you built during the run to break as many vaults as possible before running out of distance.
4. **Armory & Permanent Upgrades (Lobby Menu)**:
   * **Starting Fire Rate** (Cost: Coins)
   * **Starting Bullet Damage** (Cost: Coins)
   * **Income Multiplier** (Cost: Coins)
   * **10 Unlockable Guns** (Pistol → Dual Uzis → Shotgun → Minigun → Plasma Cannon).
5. **CrazyGames Ad Integration**:
   * *Gameplay Start*: Triggers the exact millisecond the gun starts moving down the runway.
   * *Midgame Ad*: Shows after every 3 levels during the transition screen (respects the 3-minute cooldown).
   * *Rewarded Ad*: **"3x Vault Cash"** button on the victory screen (massive click rate—players hate leaving 3x money behind).
   * *Cloud Save*: Stores player level, coin count, and unlocked guns via `CrazyGames.SDK.data`.

### Complete Milestone Roadmap (Build in Order)
* **Milestone 1 (Player & Track)**: Create a 3D or 2.5D straight track. Gun moves forward at constant speed and steers smoothly with pointer coordinates. Gun continuously fires bullets forward.
* **Milestone 2 (Math Gates & Logic)**: Spawn procedural gates on track. Detect bullet collision with gates to increase/decrease the gate's value; detect player passing through gate to update active gun stats (`fireRate`, `bulletCount`).
* **Milestone 3 (Destructible Obstacles & Finish Line)**: Add HP blocks that shrink/flash when shot. Add the End-Vault sequence where your final built gun shoots down multiplier vaults.
* **Milestone 4 (Lobby & Upgrade Economy)**: Build the HTML/CSS overlay menu with 3 upgrade buttons (Damage, Fire Rate, Income) funded by collected coins.
* **Milestone 5 (CrazyGames SDK Hookup)**: Hook up `gameplayStart()`, `gameplayStop()`, the 3x rewarded ad button, and `SDK.data` cloud save.
* **Milestone 6 (Polish & Juice)**: Screen shake when shooting the vaults, floating damage text (`+10`, `CRIT!`), glowing neon bullet materials, and punchy gunshot sounds.

# OPTION 2: *OUTBREAK: 10-MINUTE SURVIVOR* (Auto-Shooting Zombie Arena)

> **The 2-Second Concept**: You are a lone survivor in a fenced city square. You only **MOVE** (WASD or Joystick). Your character automatically shoots at the nearest zombies. Dead zombies drop glowing green XP gems. Fill the XP bar → choose 1 of 3 weapon upgrades. Survive for 10 minutes until the helicopter lands.  
> **Why Anyone Understands It**: You literally only control movement. You never have to aim or press fire. Just avoid touching enemies.

```
[MAIN MENU] ──> [ARENA BATTLE (0-10 min)] ──> [LEVEL UP (Pick 1 of 3)] ──> [EXTRACTION / DEATH]
                                                     ↑                         │
                                                     └── Collect XP Gems ──────┘
```

### Complete Game Scope (What the Full Game Contains)
1. **Controls**: WASD / Arrow Keys on PC, Virtual Touch Joystick on Mobile. Auto-target & auto-fire.
2. **Enemies (5 Types)**:
   * *Slow Walkers* (Minute 0–3): Basic cannon fodder.
   * *Fast Crawlers* (Minute 3–5): Rush the player, low HP.
   * *Armored Riot Zombies* (Minute 5–7): High HP, block bullets.
   * *Spitters* (Minute 7–9): Ranged acid attacks you must dodge.
   * *Goliaths (Bosses at Minute 3, 6, 9)*: Giant brutes with charge attacks.
3. **Weapon & Ability Synergies (Choose 1 of 3 on Level Up)**:
   * *Auto-Pistol / Shotgun*: Fires spreads in front.
   * *Rotating Energy Shields*: 2 to 4 glowing orbs spinning around you that shred anything getting too close.
   * *Lightning Drone*: Periodically strikes 3 random enemies on screen.
   * *Molotov Cocktail*: Drops fire pools on the floor that burn chasing zombies.
   * *Stat Buffs*: +15% Move Speed, +20% Pickup Radius (Magnet), +10% Max HP.
4. **Permanent Base Camp (Lobby Menu)**:
   * Coins collected from bosses buy permanent passive stats: +Health, +Armor, +Magnet Radius.
5. **CrazyGames Ad Integration**:
   * *Gameplay Start*: Triggers the moment the survivor drops into the arena.
   * *Midgame Ad*: Plays upon defeat or successful extraction (never interrupts during the 10-minute battle!).
   * *Rewarded Ad*: **"Emergency Defibrillator (Revive)"**—offers 1 second chance per run with 3 seconds of invulnerability when health reaches 0.
   * *Cloud Save*: Stores high score time, permanent base camp unlocks, and total zombie kills.

### Complete Milestone Roadmap (Build in Order)
* **Milestone 1 (Player Movement & Spawner)**: Top-down camera. Smooth 8-directional movement. Spawner generates zombies outside the screen boundaries that march directly toward player coordinates.
* **Milestone 2 (Auto-Aim & Combat)**: Script weapon to find the closest zombie in range, rotate, and fire projectiles. Implement zombie death and drop glowing XP gem objects.
* **Milestone 3 (Level Up Card System)**: When player collides with gems, fill an XP bar. When full: pause game loop, display an interactive modal with 3 random perk cards (e.g., "+1 Shield Orb", "+20% Fire Rate"), and apply selected perk.
* **Milestone 4 (Timer & Boss Waves)**: 10-minute countdown clock. Spawn mini-bosses at Minute 3, 6, and 9. At 10:00, spawn the extraction helicopter rescue zone.
* **Milestone 5 (CrazyGames SDK)**: Hook up `gameplayStart()`, 1-time Revive Rewarded Ad, and `SDK.data` cloud save.
* **Milestone 6 (Juice & Atmosphere)**: Blood splatter decals left on the ground, muzzle flash lights, zombie death groan sounds, and hit-stop pause on boss hits.

# OPTION 3: *JUNKYARD CLEANER 3D* (Arcade Idle / Vacuum Tycoon)

> **The 2-Second Concept**: You drive a small vacuum truck in a massive scrap field. Suck up colorful metal scrap and trash blocks. Drive over the Crusher to convert trash into cash. Walk onto glowing buying pads ($100 for Faster Speed, $250 to Unlock New Yard).  
> **Why Anyone Understands It**: You don't even need to shoot or dodge anything. You just drive around vacuuming things up and watching numbers go up. It is pure satisfying stress relief.

```
[VACUUM TRASH PILES] ──> [DRIVE TO CRUSHER] ──> [GET CASH] ──> [STEP ON UPGRADE TILES]
         ↑                                                              │
         └────────────────────── UNLOCK NEW ZONE ◄──────────────────────┘
```

### Complete Game Scope (What the Full Game Contains)
1. **Controls**: WASD / Arrow Keys or Mouse Drag (PC) / Touch Drag (Mobile). 
2. **Vacuum & Storage Loop**:
   * Your truck has a **Storage Capacity** (starts at 20 cubes).
   * Scrap cubes physically suck into your vacuum nozzle and stack up visibly on the back of your truck bed.
   * When capacity is full ("BAG FULL!" text appears), you drive to the glowing **Drop Zone / Shredder**.
   * Cubes shoot into the shredder, and stacks of paper cash pop out into your money counter.
3. **Zone Progression (3 Distinct Yards)**:
   * *Yard 1: Plastic & Scrap Metal* (Earns $1/cube)
   * *Yard 2: Car Demolition Lot* (Earns $5/cube, requires $1,000 to unlock gate)
   * *Yard 3: Aircraft Graveyard* (Earns $25/cube, requires $5,000 to unlock gate)
4. **Upgrade Pads (Walk onto pad to spend money)**:
   * **Vacuum Width / Suction Power**: Sucks up wider piles faster.
   * **Truck Capacity**: Increases max inventory (20 → 50 → 150 → 500).
   * **Movement Speed**: Faster truck speed.
   * **Hire AI Helpers**: Autonomous mini-cleaner bots that gather scrap and deposit it for you.
5. **CrazyGames Ad Integration**:
   * *Gameplay Start*: Triggers the moment the truck starts rolling.
   * *Midgame Ad*: Plays when unlocking a major new gate/yard (every 3+ minutes).
   * *Rewarded Ad*: **"Super Vacuum (2-Minute Boost)"**—infinite backpack capacity + 2x vacuum speed for 120 seconds.
   * *Banner Ads*: Static 300x250 banner placed neatly near the top-right upgrade zone.
   * *Cloud Save*: Stores truck stats, unlocked yards, and total cash balance.

### Complete Milestone Roadmap (Build in Order)
* **Milestone 1 (Truck Movement & Scrap Grid)**: Smooth arcade vehicle physics. Spawn a grid of 100 colorful scrap cubes with simple bounding box collision.
* **Milestone 2 (Suction Physics & Backpack Stacking)**: When truck is near scrap, pull cubes toward nozzle using `lerp`. When collected, attach cube to a grid layout on the truck bed and increment inventory count.
* **Milestone 3 (Unload Station & Currency)**: Create a designated Unload Zone tile. When truck enters, transfer cubes off the truck into the shredder one by one, play coin sound, and add cash.
* **Milestone 4 (Interactive Purchase Pads)**: Create interactive circular floor pads with floating prices (`[ Speed: $200 ]`). When player stands on pad, deduct cash gradually and apply upgrade.
* **Milestone 5 (Yard Expansion & AI Helpers)**: Add a locked gate that opens when the player fills a $1,000 buy-pad, revealing Yard 2. Add 1 AI helper bot that loops: wander → vacuum → drop off.
* **Milestone 6 (CrazyGames SDK & Juice)**: Hook up `SDK.data` cloud save, the 2-Minute Frenzy Rewarded Ad, bouncy scaling on collected cubes, and satisfying pop/cash register sounds.
